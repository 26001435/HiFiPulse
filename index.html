    How are these ratings calculated?

    These are illustrative connection-quality bands, not a guarantee. Higher download and upload are better; lower ping and jitter are better. Ratings update with live readings and settle on the final measurement.

    • Download: poor under 15, fair 15–49.99, excellent 50+ Mbps.
    • Upload: poor under 5, fair 5–19.99, excellent 20+ Mbps.
    • Ping: excellent 40 ms or less, fair above 40–120, poor above 120 ms.
    • Jitter: excellent 10 ms or less, fair above 10–30, poor above 30 ms.

    Before you press start

    What a browser test can tell you—and what it cannot.

    How much data will this test use?

    Choose Lightweight (up to about 8 MB) or Heavyweight (up to about 52 MB). These are estimates, not exact charges. On a metered connection, start with Lightweight; an interrupted or very slow test can still be recorded as incomplete rather than disappearing.

    Do I need to share my location?

    You can refuse browser location permission, but without a usable approximate fix your result cannot be placed on the map or included in location-based rankings. If you permit location, coordinates are rounded before submission; an indoor fix still cannot prove your exact room or floor.

    Is the network detected automatically?

    No. Select the network actually carrying your test traffic. Browsers cannot reliably identify a mobile carrier, and a “Campus Wi-Fi” label is self-reported. Check your device connection before starting.

    Will other students see my test immediately?

    Not necessarily. Your recent result can appear in this browser first. Shared results depend on the external submission service saving the test and the published CSV becoming available to other devices. A local save is not proof that the shared feed has updated.

    Does a fast result guarantee a good study spot?

    No. A single test reflects one network and moment. Study-space guidance needs repeated nearby observations; it cannot tell you whether a seat is free or promise that an assessment will stay connected.
